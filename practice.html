The Practice Group is an expository and discussion forum for the practical side of research related to the Reasoning and Decision-Making Lab.

Meetings examine papers, methods, implementations, and experimental questions in enough detail to understand how they work, how they are evaluated, and what their results do—and do not—show.

Weekly meeting Fridays · 2:00–3:00 PM GST (UTC+4)

01 Scope

What we may discuss

Practical questions without a fixed syllabus.

Possible sessions include LLM post-training and inference-time methods, evaluation and reliability, reproductions and ablations, code and systems walkthroughs, datasets and benchmarks, experimental design, and other applied aspects of machine-learning research.

These examples do not define a fixed syllabus. Any practical topic that connects to the lab’s research or is useful and interesting to its members may be proposed.

02 Format

How a session works

Explain the method. Examine the evidence.

A presenter chooses a paper, method, system, or experimental question and provides the background needed to understand it. Depending on the topic, the session may include an explanation of the main ideas, an examination of experimental results, a code walkthrough, a demonstration, or a discussion of implementation choices and limitations.

Questions and discussion are encouraged throughout. Presentations may be expository and do not need to concern the speaker’s own research.
