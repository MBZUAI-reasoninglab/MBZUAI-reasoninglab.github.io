Theory
Reading Group

The Theory Reading Group is an expository and discussion forum for the mathematical and conceptual foundations of research related to the Reasoning and Decision-Making Lab.

Its purpose is to develop a careful, shared understanding of important ideas rather than to give only a brief summary of a paper.

Weekly meeting Wednesdays · 2:00–3:00 PM GST (UTC+4)

01 Scope

What we may discuss

Theory selected by relevance, not by a fixed syllabus.

Possible topics include reinforcement-learning and multi-armed-bandit theory, information theory, theoretical aspects of reasoning and sequential decision-making, stochastic search, mechanism design, learning theory, and related mathematical questions.

The group does not follow a permanently fixed syllabus. A session may address any theoretical topic that connects to the lab’s research or is of interest to its members.

02 Format

How a session works

Slow down at the important step.

One or more presenters introduce the necessary background, formulate the central problem, and guide the group through the main definitions, arguments, or proofs. We slow down at technically or conceptually important steps and discuss assumptions, limitations, connections to other results, and open questions.

A session does not need to cover an entire paper. It may instead concentrate on one theorem, proof technique, model, or idea that deserves a closer examination.
